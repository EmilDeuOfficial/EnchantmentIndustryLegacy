## What is Create: Enchantment Industry Legacy?

It turns enchanting into a Create factory process. Enchantments become a fluid you can pump: strip them off
items to fill a tank, then spend that fluid to enchant other items or to copy enchanted books in bulk.

This is the **1.20.1 feature set** of Create: Enchantment Industry, brought forward to Minecraft 1.21.1 and
NeoForge. Upstream rewrote the mod for 1.21.1 around a different set of machines — this fork keeps the
Disenchanter, the Blaze Enchanter and the Printer.

This means:
- Enchantments are storable, pumpable and automatable like any other Create fluid
- Enchanted books can be mass-produced from plain books on a belt
- Hyper Experience pushes enchantments one level past their normal maximum

---

## Quick Start

1. Install [NeoForge 21.1.180+](https://neoforged.net/) for Minecraft 1.21.1
2. Download [Create 6.0.10+](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) and place it in your `mods/` folder
3. Drop `CreateEnchantmentIndustryLegacy_1.5.0_1.21.jar` into your `mods/` folder
4. Start the game
5. Craft a Disenchanter, put it over a belt, and send an enchanted item through it — the enchantments come off
   as Liquid Experience
